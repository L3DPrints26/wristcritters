Wrist Critters Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Wrist Critters is a virtual pet game for Apple Watch. It does not collect, sell or share personal information.

What stays on the watch

Your pal, your progress, your settings and your purchases are stored on your Apple Watch. We do not run any servers, accounts or analytics, and the app contains no ads and no third-party tracking.

Optional iCloud backup

If you leave iCloud backup on, a small copy of your game progress is stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's key-value storage, so a pal can come back on a new watch. We cannot see it. You can turn it off in Settings.

Steps and motion

If you allow it, the watch counts your steps so walking can earn coins in the game. The step count is used on the watch only and is never sent anywhere.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on the watch (for example, when a pal is hungry). They are created on the device. They are quiet at night and during school time.

Purchases

Optional purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Purchases ask a grown-up first.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/L3DPrints26/wristcritters/issues.
